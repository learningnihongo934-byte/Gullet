Field notes

  • Doors open when a room is empty of everything that wants to eat you.
  • Enemies flash white right before they attack. That's your cue.
  • Don't hug the walls. The walls have teeth.
  • Every kill feeds your level. Each level, once the room is clear, keep one of three things the tower swallowed (press 1, 2 or 3). Effects stack.
  • Wrapped caches are a free level. Shops sell them for teeth; feeding altars want one of your hearts.
  • Each floor ends in an organ that fights back. Beat it and climb the rope up.
  • In co-op, a fallen climber comes back when the room is cleared.
  • Auto climb lets an AI climb an infinite tower while you watch. It is only a simulation: no marrow, cards or records are kept.
  • Infinite climbs go past the heart into the liver, kidneys, tongue, nerves, eye and brain, then loop around, harder each time.
  • Stopping each of the first six organs for the first time unlocks a new weapon.
  • A cracked wall hides a secret room. Bomb it.
  • Golden enemies are tough and drop a cache. Challenge rooms throw three waves at you and pay two.
  • Climbers who came before you left notes. Collect them all to learn what the tower is.
  • Save and quit any time. You'll pick up in the same room.
